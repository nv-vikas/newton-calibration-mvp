# Isolated 25.75 mm chamfered-hole trial

This is a frozen-policy experiment, not a new training run, calibration, or
real-robot procedure. It leaves the original center-hole training job unchanged.

## Target verified from the supplied geometry

The upper-left opening carries the engraved **25.75** label. The original
`HoleBlock.usd` collision mesh already contains this bore and its chamfer; the
experiment does not enlarge the hole or manufacture a new entry surface.

- Straight bore: 25.75 mm diameter.
- Entry chamfer: 2 mm axial and radial, 45 degrees.
- Mouth: 29.75 mm diameter.
- Peg: 25 mm diameter; nominal radial bore clearance is 0.375 mm.
- Fixture-local center: approximately (-34.7412, 33.9655) mm.
- World target at the mouth: approximately (0.1152588, 0.0339655, 0.79) m.

`inspect_holes.py` produces STL cross-sections, including the actual engraved
labels. `prepare_hole_variant.py` verifies the authored USD collision vertices,
translates the held-out starting poses to the selected opening, and solves joint
configurations within the USD limits. Deterministic bounded multi-start IK is
used when the initial solve is insufficient. It never discards a failed pose
or relaxes the pose-error threshold to complete the bank.

## What changes, and what does not

`hole_variant.py` supplies a subclass of the frozen `ResinPegEnv`. The only
task changes are the target position, the derived starting-pose bank, and a
chamfer-aware geometric validity screen. Actor observations use the new target.
The fixture, robot, peg, physics settings, policy weights, observation layout,
and completion thresholds are unchanged. Initial joint configurations do change
because the arm must reach another location. This is a generalization test, not
a comparison in which only the hole diameter varies.

The validity screen samples the peg surface against an approximate analytic
description of all five openings. Actual dynamics use the original static
triangle mesh. Neither the screen nor the gravity probes certify contact forces,
real-robot safety, or physical accuracy of the unmeasured resin properties.

## Test sequence

1. Check asset/configuration hashes and checkpoint curriculum stage.
2. Run actual Newton gravity probes: centered bore must admit the peg; solid rim
   must stop it. A 1 mm offset chamfer probe is an additional diagnostic.
3. Only after the required probes pass, run four deterministic learned-policy
   episodes at seed 932701. No scripted insertion controller is used.
4. Record the first terminal result for each environment, including insertion
   depth, radial error, tilt, grasp displacement and sampled wall violation.
5. Save the checkpoint hash, target specification hash, trajectories and explicit
   completion/failure status. Preserve failed attempts in separate directories.

Success remains at least 35 mm depth, radial error no more than 0.35 mm, tilt no
more than 1 degree, and a 0.25 s hold without a flagged invalid state. Four
exploratory simulated episodes do not establish a reliable success rate or
sim-to-real transfer. Poses are sampled from a finite held-out bank; repeated
poses are possible.

## Tools and output

- `prepare_hole_variant.py --prepared DIR --output spec.json --stage 1`
- `try_hole_variant.py --prepared DIR --run TRAINING_DIR --checkpoint CHECKPOINT
  --variant spec.json --output NEW_RESULT_DIR --headless`
- Run inside the pinned `flexiv-resin-insertion:20260916-v24` image with the
  original prepared assets and training directory mounted read-only. Mount the
  helpers outside the frozen source directory to preserve its fingerprint.
- Use the explicit result/status JSON files, not Docker exit code alone.
- `test_hole_variant.py` covers the supplemental geometry screen; it does not
  substitute for runtime collision probes or policy evaluation.
- The existing `render_policy.py` still targets the original center hole. Its
  old video must not be presented as a recording of this 25.75 mm experiment.
  A new capture must use the variant environment and identify the checkpoint,
  starting pose, target and observed outcome explicitly.

Preparation attempt `hole2575_v1` failed its initial single-start IK check before
any physics or policy trials. `hole2575_v2` adds bounded alternate IK seeds and
preserves the failed preparation separately.

## Completed exploratory result — September 16, 2026

`hole2575_v2` tested checkpoint `model_731.pt` (732 updates): **3/4** distinct
held-out starts inserted successfully, reaching 38.93–39.32 mm in 2.93–3.03 s.
The fourth never entered and moved away until the 23.967 s timeout. All required
collision probes and the optional offset chamfer probe passed. This is a small
simulation-only result, not a reliable success rate or real transfer claim.

The exact checkpoint, helper sources, specification and results were copied to
the ignored repo directory `output/resin_insertion/hole2575_v2/`. Consult its
`RESULT_SUMMARY.md` and `results/result.json` for the complete record. The original
training container remained unchanged. No new video was captured in this test.

A subsequent requested video was captured in
`output/resin_insertion/video_hole2575_policy731_v2/output/policy_progress.mp4`.
It is a fresh learned-policy run using the selected trial-3 starting condition,
not an animation of the saved trajectories. It passed the insertion checks at
39.214 mm depth and 2.933 s. Playback is 0.5x with a 2-second final-frame hold;
the caption explicitly identifies the selected example and earlier 3/4 result.
